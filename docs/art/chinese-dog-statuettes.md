# Chinese dog statuettes — museum photographs

Checked and downloaded on 2026-10-29. These are historical depictions of dogs,
not museum identifications of the modern Shar-Pei breed.

Both object pages label their photographs **Public Domain**. The Metropolitan
Museum of Art releases its Open Access images under **CC0**, allowing downloading,
sharing, modification and commercial use:
https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access

The museum's API also returned `isPublicDomain: true` for both objects.

## Figure of a Dog

- Object page: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/42361
- API: https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/42361
- Accession: 1991.253.1
- China, Eastern Han dynasty (25–220 CE).
- Earthenware with dark green glaze.
- Museum credit: Gift of Stanley Herzman, in memory of Adele Herzman, 1991.
- Photo source: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/web-large/DT6718.jpg
- Local asset: `public/assets/history/han-dog-met-42361.webp` (500 × 625).

The downloaded museum photographs were converted to WebP at quality 84, without
cropping or altering the objects. Together they are about 61 KB. They are loaded
lazily inside the dog detail card. Captions link to the museum records and name
the museum, date, accession number and rights status.
